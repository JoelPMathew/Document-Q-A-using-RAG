# ACME Corp Employee Handbook

## 1. Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days a week. The core working hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST, during which all employees must be online and available for synchronous communication. To request fully remote status, an employee must submit a formal request to HR at least 30 days in advance. 

## 2. Hardware Reimbursement
The company provides a one-time stipend of $500 for home office setup. Additionally, employees can expense up to $50 per month for home internet. Receipts must be submitted via the Confluence portal by the 5th of every month.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month. PTO rolls over to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any accrued PTO beyond 10 days at the end of the year will be forfeited. Note that managers require 2 weeks notice for any PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Security Protocols
All internal communications must occur over the approved company Slack workspace. Email should only be used for external facing communications. Employees must enable 2FA on all company-related accounts. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.
