# IT Support FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
You can reset your password by visiting https://sso.nexus.ai/reset. You will need your employee ID and access to your registered mobile device for MFA.

## What should I do if my laptop is stolen?
Report the theft immediately to the IT Security team at security@nexus.ai and file a police report. IT will remotely wipe the device.

## How do I request new software?
Submit a ticket through the Jira Service Desk under the "Software Request" category. Approval from your manager is required for paid licenses.
