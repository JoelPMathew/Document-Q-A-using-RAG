# Nexus Neural Systems - Employee Handbook

## Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Coordination with the team lead is required by Monday of each week.

## Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Security Protocol
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all internal systems. Sharing of credentials is strictly prohibited.
