# SOP: Incident Response

## Phase 1: Identification
Identify the scope and severity of the incident. High-severity incidents involve data breaches or system-wide downtime.

## Phase 2: Containment
Isolate affected systems to prevent further spread. For network-level threats, disable the affected VLANs immediately.

## Phase 3: Eradication
Remove the root cause of the incident. This may involve patching vulnerabilities or removing malware.

## Phase 4: Recovery
Restore systems from clean backups. Monitor for any signs of recurrence.
